# BEONx

> BEONx is an AI-powered hotel Revenue Management System (RMS) — revenue management software for hotels, hotel chains and resorts. It helps hoteliers optimize pricing, forecast demand, and maximize total profitability by unifying all their data in a single platform. BEONx is a hospitality technology company headquartered in Salamanca, Spain.

BEONx provides revenue management software and a revenue management system for the hotel industry, combining AI-based demand forecasting, automated and personalized price optimization, and business intelligence reporting. Its approach, called "Sustainable Profitability", goes beyond traditional metrics such as RevPAR and ADR to optimize a hotel's profit across all revenue streams. A signature, proprietary feature is the HQi (Hotel Quality Index), the only index in the hotel market that measures overall hotel quality to understand competitive positioning and price elasticity. BEONx integrates with the leading PMS, Channel Managers, Booking Tools and BI systems.
